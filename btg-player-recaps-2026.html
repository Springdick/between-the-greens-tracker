Six shooters, eighteen weeks, three seasons of history. One good word and one bad one for each of you.
The team, three seasons deep
Brad dug out the 2024 and 2025 year-end sheets back in April, which means for the first time we can look at this team over three seasons instead of one. Two things jump out, and neither is what you'd guess from the standings.

One: the three men who improved are the three who were worst in 2024. Brad, Tim and Glenn have gone up every single year — Brad +1.89 and Tim +1.87 across two seasons, from the bottom of the sheet. The three who declined — Bill, Dave, Kevin — were the top three.

Two: this team has compressed, hard. In 2024 the gap between our best and worst Singles average was 4.11 birds. In 2025, 2.28. This year: 1.67. Two years ago we were a team with a couple of shooters and some passengers. Now there is barely a bird and a half between first and last. The team average went 20.68 → 21.72 → 21.27 — so 2025 was our best-scoring year, and 2026 was our tightest.

That's the good news and the bad news in one sentence: nobody here is carrying anybody any more, and nobody here is running away with it either.
